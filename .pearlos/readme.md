# PearlOS Knowledge Store

This `.pearlos/` folder contains the organization knowledge managed by PearlOS.

## Structure
- `voice.md`: Structured tone-of-voice guidelines.
- `truths.md`: Structured business truths and constraints.
- `knowledge/`: Derived organization role playbooks.
- `artifacts/`: Structured outputs created by PearlOS agents.
- `capture/`: Raw inputs, logs, and information gathered for processing.
- `DECISIONS.md`: Log of architectural and business decisions.
